    Immediate Life-Safety Priority

    Experiencing a Medical Emergency?

    Do NOT wait for an AI response or use a symptom checker if you or someone near you has potentially life-threatening symptoms. Contact your local emergency services immediately or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

    Call Emergency (911 / 112 / 999) Available 24/7 • Local Emergency Dispatch

    Critical Warning Signs Requiring Immediate Care

    Seek emergency medical assistance right away if any of the following occur:

    Severe Chest Pain or Pressure

    Squeezing, fullness, or pain in the center of the chest, especially if radiating to the arm, back, neck, jaw, or accompanied by cold sweats or nausea.

    Difficulty Breathing

    Sudden shortness of breath, gasping for air, inability to speak full sentences, bluish lips or face, or severe wheezing.

    FAST

    Stroke Warning Signs

    • F — Face: Drooping or numbness on one side
    • A — Arms: Weakness or drift in one arm
    • S — Speech: Slurred or strange speech
    • T — Time: Call emergency services immediately

    Unconsciousness or Fainting

    Sudden loss of responsiveness, fainting, confusion, seizures, or sudden severe headache ("worst headache of your life").

    Severe Allergic Reaction

    Swelling of the lips, tongue, or throat, hives spreading rapidly, dizziness, or trouble swallowing (Anaphylaxis).

    While Waiting for Help

    Emergency Dispatcher Checklist

    • State your exact location and phone number first.
    • Describe the person's age, consciousness, and breathing.
    • Mention when symptoms started and any known allergies.
    • Stay on the line until the dispatcher tells you to hang up.
